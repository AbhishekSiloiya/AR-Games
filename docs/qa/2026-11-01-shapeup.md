# Shape Up staged progression / 1 November 2026

## Change

- Five authored eight-pose schedules now replace the single repeated schedule. Stage 1 retains open beginner shapes; stage 2 adds bent arms; stage 3 adds asymmetric arms and wider standing shapes; stages 4 and 5 combine shallow standing leg bends with asymmetric arm poses. No animal-pose feature was added.
- Every consecutive target requires a change beyond the existing generous matching tolerance. Holding the previous target cannot automatically match the next one. The required 0.45-second continuous genuine hold remains unchanged.
- The earned result is shown for 0.8 seconds, followed by 0.45 seconds of reset on stages 1–2. Stages 3–5 use 0.7/0.35 seconds. This replaces the previous 1.2/1.4 seconds. During reset, the title and 3D coach preview the actual upcoming target.
- Current stage name, focus, target order, match guidance, GPU coach poses and result notes all use the same quest schedule.
- Added explicit earned FLOW bonus / 3, 5 and 8-chain milestone ribbons and a visible next-bonus goal. Existing accuracy, stars, independent player scoring and bonus amounts remain unchanged: eight perfect poses earn 1,165 points and 24 pose stars.
- The natural live camera, tracking freshness gate, limb confidence checks, pause hold reset, paired independence, reduced motion and lifecycle cleanup remain intact.

## Practical limits

The fake camera confirms mapping, matching rules, rendering and UI lifecycle, not physical-camera recognition quality. Stage 3–5 pose variety and comfortable shallow stance ranges need physical player acceptance across heights, mobility and camera positions. No new floor, deep squat, jumping or one-legged balance requirement was introduced.
