## Mechanics

- Goalie Stage 1/practice remain near left/right. Stage 2 introduces both upper corners; Stage 3 asks for full upper stretches (x ±4.2 / y2.25 before layout/reach scaling), alternating low resets. Stages 4–5 add more readable ±1.15–1.4 unit bends and upper-corner combinations. Target/ring centres remain the true flight endpoint. Existing speed ramp and scoring are preserved.
- Comfort reach now scales vertical endpoints as well as horizontal endpoints. Solo and paired full/comfort targets remain inside the mapped hand range. Either glove can save the true high endpoint.
- Jack steady aim commits after95ms (previously140ms); held poses still fire once. Gradual raises at15/30/60/120fps, missing/hidden wrists, manual reset and long gaps still prevent premature firing. Normal travelling pulses move17units/s (previously11), Starfall19units/s; no hit is awarded at launch.
- A descending ship at y1.25 now begins a visible950ms dive toward its own crew pilot's actual head/chest shield target. Swept contact with that target causes shield damage. Ships no longer despawn/damage a pilot far above its body. A targeted player becoming untracked freezes that dive; a crew with nobody fresh does not advance combat.
- Each crew has a1.25s shield guard after contact, preventing simultaneous ships from draining multiple shields per frame. A real third hit visibly leaves0/3 shields during a2.5s regroup, then restores3. Shared/versus crew ownership and the existing5-point regroup cost are preserved. Unshot attackers are never counted as player kills/perfect clears.
- Real contacts trigger a local shield ring around the actual pilot and per-player HUD hit/remaining-shield feedback. No fullscreen tint. The compact .56/.46 pilot and existing earned/no-self-charge Starfall mechanics remain.
