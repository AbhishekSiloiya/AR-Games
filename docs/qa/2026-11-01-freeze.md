# Freeze dance effects — 1 November 2026

## Change

The existing Rizzquake skinned dance partner is now 76% of its previous size. It retains the same tracked arm/leg motion, pose demonstration and material. Real, fresh, present-player movement samples now earn short confetti/sparkle bursts around the partner during the dance phase, starting before the first freeze. A small faceted disco ball rotates in proportion to that movement and a dedicated warm partner spotlight becomes visible. A still or absent player earns none of these effects. The new effects never modify the studio's score, dance eligibility, pose matching, hold awards or chains.

The live camera remains unfiltered. Particle positions explicitly exclude its entire rectangle with a safety margin; the atmosphere shader discards that same region before drawing the partner light. The small ball, cable, glints and partner occupy the left-side studio area. Existing decorative tiny glints were removed so dance-earned sparkles are distinguishable.

Freeze stops the ball and clears dance particles/partner light. Pause holds the complete state through the existing audio/game clock. Effects-off, lean performance mode and reduced-motion disable emitted particles, light and ball rotation, while the original movement scoring continues. All meshes/materials are round-owned and dispose with the existing scene lifetime.
