# Shape Up Pose Club implementation plan

**Goal:** Build the approved single polished live-AR round: clear target, useful match guidance, an immediate earned match and a short reset, with adult-friendly visual quality at the Jab/Slice benchmark.
**Architecture:** Eight authored, readable full-body poses in a deliberate easy-to-complex first round. Keep the existing per-pose stars/combo/points and source-landmark visibility guards, but use a Shape Up-specific variable phase clock so genuinely locked poses bank immediately and move on after a result/reset. Two players share the reference/timer, bank independently, and wait for the other player or the timer. One actual skinned Rizzquake demonstrates target limb angles; no raster puppets or tower sprites. A bright unfiltered live-camera surface is the main playing area; small wrist/ankle markers report limb matches without hiding the person. Shared tracking, menus and other games stay unchanged.
**Visual thesis:** A sunlit cream/sea-glass coastal pose pavilion, one full-size Rizzquake on a shallow dais to the right, player view on the left, one large target name, a compact elegant timer, earned 8-step progress and a readable match/hold cue. Target interpolation, hold fill and genuine match celebrations have purpose; reduced motion/effects disable secondary decoration. No busy dark overlays or repeated text.
**Alternatives:** Overlaying a full opaque target on the player would hide their body; the existing raster target and tower add visual clutter. Choose the side-by-side live person + single articulated coach. Authored first-round poses avoid unreadable crossed-arm or unbalanced random openings; bespoke five environments remain later work.
