# Goalie: five-stage progression

User approved the stage roadmap after hands-on acceptance of the first-round stadium. Keep one ball at a time, the existing responsive glove contact, Comfortable/Full reach, independent paired play and the right-hand menus. Preview only.

Visual thesis: one coherent premium stadium evolves from daylight through sunset to a bright floodlit final; the original Rizzquake remains the active striker and reacts to genuine saves.
Content plan: stage selection explains one new movement; the countdown demonstrates its rings; play shows a single shot cue and a compact score/star objective; results name the drill and reward an earned replay or next-stage unlock.
Interaction thesis: stage-specific left/right, corner, high/low, curved and mixed patterns teach before testing. The ring remains truthful. Saves compress gloves, deflect the ball and trigger brief streak celebrations outside the aiming area.

Chosen approach: authored deterministic patterns plus existing modest flight-time ramp. Speed-only difficulty was rejected by the approved overview; random rapid-fire is outside this pass. Stage1 near left/right; stage2 wider corners; stage3 high/low combinations; stage4 readable curved approaches; stage5 mixed final showdown with a final10-second sequence. Each shot freezes its chosen pattern, duration and curve at preparation, including when crossing the finale boundary. Practice remains two slow, simple shots with no score.

Use three environment assets (daylight, sunset, floodlit), not five unrelated worlds. Stage-specific original synth arrangements and crowd swells build energy without changing tracking or adding flashing effects. High-contrast equipment/UI stay readable at night. Reduced motion removes secondary celebrations and decorative movement while retaining ball/striker actions needed to play.

Existing60s clock and fixed cadence stay;2 stars unlock the next stage. Live progress uses existing star goals; best scores remain per stage/profile. No adaptive speed, new currencies, extra characters or production promotion.

Acceptance: distinct intended shot patterns including actual curves and exact ring endpoints; no midpoint curve/ramp changes; all20 perfect-round combinations can reach3 stars; honest stage preview and actual scene match; practice/pause/absence and paired isolation preserved; responsive readable HUD; stage audio cleanup. Browser captures at stages1/3/5 plus reduced motion, results/unlocks and existing Jab/menu regression. Real camera comfort/audio balance still require user playtesting.
