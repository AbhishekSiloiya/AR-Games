# Freeze studio benchmark: design and implementation plan

**Approved direction:** Next game after Goalie: the real player bright/prominent, one original skinned Rizzquake dance partner, clear music stop, earned holds/celebrations and later-stage pose challenges. First focused studio upgrade; preview only, separate branch from the now-updated Goalie. Keep Jab/Goalie/menus.

**Visual thesis:** Bright premium cream/coral dance studio with a tactile chrome disco ball, restrained rhythmic floor lighting and one active3D Rizzquake beside a large unfiltered mirrored live-camera window. Full camera exposure; no dark overlay, cutout puppet or floor blocking the player.
**Content:** One headline DANCE/FREEZE, one upcoming pose, compact score/hold progress and earned streak feedback. Briefing shows selected stage and music choice; results clean holds/best chain/dance work. Actual camera is hero; character helps play rather than occupying space.
**Interaction:** During dance Rizzquake immediately follows confident tracked arms/legs/torso; during pose challenges it demonstrates the requested pose and holds still with the environment when the music stops. Independent paired scores share one coach; all cues have text equivalents and reduced-motion behavior. Same original procedural soundtrack plus existing CC0 alternatives, gated through shared pauseable audio clock.
