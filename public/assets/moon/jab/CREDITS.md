# MOON Jab graphics

- Rizzquake identity reference: user-supplied `output/characters/rizzquake.png`, preserved untouched. Inclusion here does not establish rights to unrelated third-party reference images.
- `rizzquake.glb`: original mesh, skin weights, face joints and six reference clips authored for MOON in `tools/build-rizzquake.py`. Regenerate with Python 3; no Blender or external character service needed. Runtime presentation/recoil uses IK and game-clock joint control; the stored reference clips are not played by the current round.
- `wool.webp`: original material swatch generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-27. Converted from generated PNG to WebP without semantic edits. Source: exec-a7fb72dd-cacc-4fcc-a3a6-c3db385d1604.png. Used as a tintable surface texture on the 3D model, not a character sprite.
- `pavilion.webp`: original empty architectural backplate generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-27. Converted from generated PNG to WebP without semantic edits. Source: exec-efefab0b-e670-4292-b6c4-8c8b849ff182.png. Character, equipment and floor shadows render separately in real time. The distant architecture/city are baked artwork, not fully modelled geometry.
- Gloves, mitts, contact/reward graphics, procedural woven normal data and shadow-catching mat are original code-native geometry/materials.
- Switch Sports informed the direction for clarity and sporting presentation; no Nintendo characters, logos or assets were used.

## Material prompt

Seamless tileable PBR base-color texture for a premium stylized 3D game character made of fine short dense wool felt. Square full-frame material swatch, neutral warm ivory / very light gray fibers, extremely subtle tonal variation, small soft interwoven fuzzy fibers, high thread density. Orthographic straight-on flat scan, even diffuse lighting, no highlights, no shadows, no vignetting, no objects, no perspective, no text, no seams, no edges. Not chunky knitted loops, not woven basket, not carpet tufts, not hair. Soft tactile microtexture, suitable for tinting green, purple, yellow and charcoal in a realtime game material.

## Pavilion prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: empty architectural environment backplate for MOON Jab, a premium stylized realtime 3D sports game. Landscape 16:9. A beautifully designed contemporary sunlit sports pavilion, warm ivory architectural walls and beams, very tall elegant glazing with soft daylight, subtle city and palms through the windows, fine warm wood details and a muted teal sports mat. Sophisticated Nintendo-Switch-Sports-like clarity with original architectural design, soft physically coherent lighting, appealing restrained colors and tactile high quality materials. Camera at player eye height, facing directly into the pavilion, level horizon and comfortable approximately 45 degree vertical field of view, no dramatic perspective, floor/back-wall junction about 64 percent down the image, a generously open central practice area. Far left a discreet training bag and a small weight rack; far right a low wood bench, water bottle and distant palms. Architecture and objects stay peripheral. Teal floor fills lower 36 percent and has only minimal cream court boundary lines at outer edges; no central circle. Center area has no object. Clear warm daytime exposure, calm soft shadows, no gloomy ceiling, no green cast, no haze, no harsh sun patches, no neon. This is environment art to place behind a separately rendered moving 3D coach and foreground gloves. ABSOLUTELY NO characters, no people, no creatures, no gloves, no boxing mitts, no floating objects, no text, no logo, no HUD, no poster graphics, no border, no screen/device mockup. One empty playable sports space, not marketing key art.
